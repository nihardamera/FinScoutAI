## FlexiPay India - Customer Grievance Redressal Policy

### Channels
- Customers can raise a complaint in the app, by email to support@flexipay.example or on the toll-free helpline (9 am to 9 pm, all days).
- Every complaint gets a reference number by SMS within 1 hour.

### Resolution timelines
- Complaints are resolved within 30 days. Unresolved complaints are escalated to the Principal Nodal Officer after 15 days.
- If a complaint is rejected or not resolved within 30 days, the customer is told they may approach the RBI Ombudsman under the Integrated Ombudsman Scheme.

### Failed transactions
- A failed UPI transaction where the account was debited is reversed by T+1 day. If it is not, FlexiPay pays the customer Rs 100 per day of delay, as required by RBI's turnaround-time framework.

### Unauthorised transactions
- Customers have zero liability for an unauthorised wallet transaction reported within 3 days.
- A reported unauthorised transaction is credited back (shadow reversal) within 10 days.

## FlexiPay India - Customer Onboarding and KYC Policy

### Who can be onboarded
- FlexiUPI and FlexiWallet are offered only to resident Indian individuals aged 18 or over.
- Non-resident Indians (NRIs), Persons of Indian Origin (PIOs), companies, partnerships and foreign investors are not onboarded. Applications from them are declined at the eligibility step.

### Identity verification
- Default route: Aadhaar-based e-KYC with OTP, plus PAN verification against the Income Tax database.
- Customers who want limits above Rs 50,000 per month complete Video KYC (V-CIP) with a trained FlexiPay official. The official sees the original PAN card on camera and records the session.
- Accepted officially valid documents (OVDs): Aadhaar, passport, driving licence, voter ID card.
- Where offline Aadhaar verification is not possible, an operations officer compares the uploaded copy of the OVD with the original during the video call and records the comparison in the KYC system.

### Periodic updation
- KYC is refreshed every 10 years for low-risk customers, every 8 years for medium-risk and every 2 years for high-risk customers.

### Records
- KYC records are kept for 5 years after the customer relationship ends, as required by the Prevention of Money-laundering Act, 2002.

### Ownership
- Policy owner: Chief Compliance Officer. Reviewed once a year by the Board Risk Committee.

## FlexiPay India - Fraud Risk Management Policy

### Authentication
- The FlexiUPI app is bound to the customer's SIM and device. Changing device requires a fresh SMS verification and a 24-hour cooling period with a lower limit of Rs 5,000.
- Wallet loads by card use an additional factor of authentication (card OTP).

### Monitoring
- A rules engine screens every transaction in real time for velocity, amount and device anomalies. Alerts are reviewed by the fraud operations team within 4 hours.

### Reporting
- Suspicious transaction reports are filed with FIU-IND within 7 days of the suspicion being established.
- Frauds are reported to the RBI and, where needed, to the police. Fraud data is reviewed monthly by the Risk Committee.

### Staff
- The fraud risk function reports to the Chief Risk Officer and is independent of business teams.
